# What's New

## Improvements

- Major download stability and recovery improvements, especially for large and long-running downloads.
- Improved Archive.org/TLS handling, automatic failover, stalled-transfer detection, and download diagnostics.
- Added ZIP extraction recovery with better integrity checks and options to keep or delete failed downloads.
- Improved error reporting and diagnostic information.
- Added left analog stick support for UI navigation.
- Improved controls in app details and link selection.
- Circle can now quickly clear active catalog filters.
- Additional stability fixes, UI improvements, and catalog updates.
